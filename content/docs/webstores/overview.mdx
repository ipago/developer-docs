**Webstores** are 2Pay's hosted storefronts and the main way to sell. They are the
foundation the other products build on.

## How it works

- Every store is hosted by 2Pay on its own **subdomain**, for example
  `yourstore.2pay.gg`.
- You create and manage everything your store sells, products, pricing, and
  theming, in the admin panel at **app.2pay.gg**.
- 2Pay is the **Merchant of Record**, so payments, global tax, fraud, and
  payouts are handled for you.

## What you can sell

Anything you define as a product in your panel: ranks and perks, in-game
currency, crates and keys, digital downloads, and bundles.

## Get started

1. Create your store and define your products in **app.2pay.gg**.
2. Theme the store and publish it on your `*.2pay.gg` subdomain.
3. Share the link and start selling.
