Welcome to the **2Pay** documentation. 2Pay is a Merchant of Record for digital
goods and game servers: you sell, and we handle payments, global tax, fraud, and
payouts.

## Where to start

- **Webstores** — launch a hosted, branded store in minutes.
- **Checkout** — embed a complete checkout into your own site, app, or game.
- **Developer API** — build directly on the resource-oriented 2Pay API.

Continue to the [API overview](/docs/api-overview) to see the resource model.
