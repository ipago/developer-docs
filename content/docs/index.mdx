Welcome to the **2Pay** documentation. 2Pay is a Merchant of Record for digital
goods and game servers: you sell, and we handle payments, global tax, fraud, and
payouts.

Everything is built on one shared base, with products defined and managed in your
admin panel at **app.2pay.gg**. There are three ways to sell:

## Products

<Cards>
  <Card title="Webstores" href="/docs/webstores/overview">
    Hosted storefronts on a 2Pay subdomain. The fastest way to start selling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Checkout" href="/docs/checkout/overview">
    Embed the 2Pay checkout directly into your own site or app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Developer API" href="/docs/developer-api/overview">
    A headless API to manage your products and track delivery.
  </Card>
</Cards>
