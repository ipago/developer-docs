**Checkout** lets you embed the 2Pay checkout directly on your own site or app,
so buyers pay without leaving your page. It is 2Pay's equivalent of a drop-in
checkout script.

## Important: products come first

Checkout sells the products you have **already defined in your admin panel at
app.2pay.gg**. It references those existing products, it does **not** create new
ones.

This means the typical flow is:

1. Define your products in **app.2pay.gg**.
2. Embed the 2Pay checkout on your site and point it at those products.
3. The buyer pays in an embedded flow, with no redirect.
4. 2Pay settles the payment and handles tax and fraud as Merchant of Record.

## When to use Checkout

Use Checkout when you already have your own website, app, or game client and
want the purchase to happen there, while still letting 2Pay own payments and
compliance.
