---
title: Payouts
description: How your earnings become a payout, and the hold window before funds are available.
---

When a sale is paid, your net (the price minus fees) is credited to your store's
balance. That balance becomes **available** after a short hold window, then can be
paid out.

## Available vs pending

- **Pending**: recent earnings still inside the payout hold window.
- **Available**: matured earnings you can withdraw now.

The hold protects against refunds and disputes landing right after a sale. You can
see both figures for your store in the panel.

## Requesting a payout

From the panel, request a payout of your available balance to your configured
payout details. A minimum amount may apply. You will get a confirmation once the
payout is sent.

<Callout type="info">
  Tax collected on your sales is never part of your balance. 2Pay remits it as
  your Merchant of Record, so your payout is your revenue after fees, nothing to
  reconcile.
</Callout>
