---
title: Payouts
description: How your earnings become a payout, where the money goes, and who sends it.
---

When a sale is paid, your net (the price excluding VAT, minus fees) is credited to
your store's balance. That balance becomes **available** after a hold window, and
then you can pay it out to your bank account.

## Available vs pending

- **Pending**: recent earnings still inside the payout hold window (7 days by
  default).
- **Available**: matured earnings you can withdraw now.

The hold protects against refunds and disputes landing right after a sale. You
can see both figures under **Balances** in the panel.

## Add your bank account

Payouts go by bank transfer to an IBAN. Add it under **Balances**, on the
**Payouts** tab, with the account holder's name, the IBAN and, optionally, the
BIC. The bank account belongs to your account, so it is shared by all its
stores.

- **Only the account owner can add or change it.** Admins and members see it
  masked to the last four digits.
- **Every change is announced by email**, so an unexpected change does not go
  unnoticed.
- A payout keeps the bank details it was requested with. Changing the bank
  account later does not redirect a payout that is already on its way.

## Requesting a payout

From **Balances**, on the **Payouts** tab, request a payout of some or all of
your available balance. Owners and admins can request payouts. A live payout
needs:

- a **verified account** (see [Identity verification](/creators/getting-started/identity-verification)),
- a **bank account** on file.

Payouts are not available while a store is suspended.

## What happens next

2Pay sends the bank transfer to your IBAN. When it has gone out, 2Pay marks the
payout as **paid** and records the bank transfer reference, which you can see on
the payout, and you get an email. If the transfer comes back, the payout is marked
failed, you are emailed, and the amount returns to your balance.

<Callout type="info">
  Tax collected on your sales is never part of your balance. 2Pay remits it as
  your Merchant of Record, so your payout is your revenue after fees, nothing to
  reconcile.
</Callout>
