iPago Sp. z o.o. is the seller to the player. It buys the licence from you (the
supplier) and resells it in its own name. **There is no contract between you and
the player.** iPago handles refunds, complaints, and chargebacks.

## Refunds

Owners and admins can refund a paid order from its page under **Transactions**.
A refund from the panel is always **full**: it returns the whole order to the
player, VAT included. The player is paid back by iPago.

Need to return only part of an order? Contact iPago support. Partial refunds are
issued by iPago staff on request.

Refunds you have issued are listed on the **Refunds** tab of **Transactions**.

Returning money to a player does not, by itself, change the purchase price. iPago
may sell at any price and still owes the purchase price. A refund is not a
separate fee.

A partially refunded order stays **paid** until the whole amount has been
returned, at which point it becomes **refunded**.

## Disputes (chargebacks)

If a player disputes a charge with their bank, iPago handles it as the seller.
You get an email alert, the dispute appears under **Inquiries**, and you can
**submit evidence** (what was delivered, proof, and context) there before the
deadline. iPago uses that evidence when it responds.

A **€15** dispute fee reduces the purchase price **only when the supplier is at
fault**. If the supplier is not at fault, the dispute fee does not reduce the
purchase price. The fee is not taken on every dispute, and it is not taken merely
because a dispute was opened or lost.

See [Pricing](/creators/pricing/fees-billing) for how the fee sits on the weekly
invoice.

<Callout type="warn">
  Submit evidence promptly. Disputes have hard deadlines set by the card networks,
  and a missed deadline is treated as a loss.
</Callout>
