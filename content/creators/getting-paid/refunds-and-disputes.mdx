## Refunds

Owners and admins can refund a paid order from its page under **Transactions**.
A refund from the panel is always **full**: it returns the whole order. The
refund reverses the sale in your ledger and returns the money to the buyer's
payment method. Because 2Pay
is the Merchant of Record, the tax portion is handled for you: the buyer gets back
what they paid, VAT included, and the VAT is removed from what 2Pay remits on your
behalf.

Need to return only part of an order? Contact 2Pay support. Partial refunds are
issued by 2Pay staff on request.

Refunds you have issued are listed on the **Refunds** tab of **Transactions**.

### What a refund costs you

**Fees are not returned.** Your balance is reduced by the order amount excluding
VAT, while the platform fee and the payment fee on the original sale stay
charged. The cost of taking the payment was already incurred, and it is not
recoverable when the money goes back. No extra fee is charged for the refund
itself.

A simplified example, on a 100.00 sale (excluding VAT) with a 5% platform fee and
a 2.9% payment fee:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| You were credited at the sale | 92.10 |
| Your balance is reduced by the refund | 100.00 |
| Net effect of selling and then refunding | **-7.90** |

A partial refund issued by 2Pay works the same way per slice: it returns that
part of the amount and keeps the fees. A partially refunded order stays **paid**
until the whole amount has been returned, at which point it becomes **refunded**.

## Disputes (chargebacks)

If a buyer disputes a charge with their bank, 2Pay handles the process as the
seller. You get an email alert, the dispute appears under **Inquiries**, and you can **submit evidence**
(a description of what was delivered, proof, and context) there before the
deadline.

- When a dispute opens, the **€15.00** dispute fee is taken from your balance,
  win or lose.
- If the dispute is **won**, the funds stand.
- If it is **lost**, the sale is reversed from your balance the same way a full
  refund is.

See [Fees & billing](/creators/pricing/fees-billing) for the dispute fee.

<Callout type="warn">
  Submit evidence promptly. Disputes have hard deadlines set by the card networks,
  and a missed deadline is treated as a loss.
</Callout>
