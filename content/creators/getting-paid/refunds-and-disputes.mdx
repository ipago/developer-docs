## Refunds

You can refund a paid order from the panel. The refund reverses the sale in your
ledger, and the buyer is notified. Because 2Pay is the Merchant of Record, the tax
portion is handled for you: the buyer gets back what they paid, VAT included, and
the VAT is removed from what 2Pay remits on your behalf.

### What a refund costs you

**Fees are not returned.** Your balance is reduced by the full amount refunded,
while the platform fee and the payment fee on the original sale stay charged. This
is how card processing works everywhere: the cost of taking the payment was already
incurred, and it is not recoverable when the money goes back.

A worked example, on a 100.00 sale with a 5% platform fee and a 2.9% payment fee:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| You were credited at the sale | 92.10 |
| Your balance is reduced by the refund | 100.00 |
| Net effect of selling and then refunding | **-7.90** |

**Partial refunds work the same way, per slice.** Refunding half returns half the
amount and keeps the fees. Refunding an order in several pieces costs you exactly
the same as refunding it in one go, so you never need to think about how to split
it.

A partially refunded order stays **paid** until the whole amount has been returned,
at which point it becomes **refunded**.

## Disputes (chargebacks)

If a buyer disputes a charge with their bank, 2Pay handles the process. You will be
alerted, and you can **submit evidence** (a description of what was delivered,
proof, and context) from the panel before the deadline.

- If the dispute is **won**, the funds stand.
- If it is **lost**, the amount is reversed, and a dispute fee may apply.

<Callout type="warn">
  Submit evidence promptly. Disputes have hard deadlines set by the card networks,
  and a missed deadline is treated as a loss.
</Callout>
