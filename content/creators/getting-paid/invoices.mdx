---
title: Invoice payment
description: How iPago pays the purchase price on a weekly self-billing invoice.
---

iPago Sp. z o.o. buys licences and other digital content from you (the supplier)
and resells them to players in its own name. There is no contract between you and
the player. What iPago owes you is the **purchase price**, paid by **invoice
payment** on a weekly invoice.

## What you are paid

For each sale you receive what the player paid before VAT, minus two things:

- iPago's **margin**, 5% of that price by default. It is stored per store, so a
  different rate can be agreed.
- The **card cost**, 2.9% of what the player paid (VAT included) plus €0.30 per
  payment.

Player VAT is not deducted from you. iPago is the seller to the player and
remits that VAT. See [Margin & card cost](/creators/pricing/fees-billing) for a
worked example.

## Weekly invoice

Each week iPago issues a **self-billing purchase invoice** for the licences it
bought, under article 106d of the Polish VAT Act. The invoice lists the purchase
prices for that week.

The invoice states the **payment term**. iPago pays the invoice by its due date,
to the bank account on your account.

There is no amount set aside for you to withdraw, no hold after a sale, and no
payout you request. The weekly invoice replaces that.

## Bank account

Invoice payments go by bank transfer to an IBAN. Add it on the account in the
panel, with the account holder's name, the IBAN and, optionally, the BIC. The
bank account belongs to your account, so it is shared by all its stores.

- **Only the account owner can add or change it.** Admins and members see it
  masked to the last four digits.
- **Every change is announced by email**, so an unexpected change does not go
  unnoticed.

A live store needs a verified account before iPago pays an invoice. See
[Identity verification](/creators/getting-started/identity-verification).

<Callout type="info">
  Some API routes are still named `/v1/balance` and `/v1/payouts`. Those names
  are the current API, not how you are paid. Settlement is this invoice.
</Callout>
