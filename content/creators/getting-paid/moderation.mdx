---
title: Moderation
description: Handle refunds and disputes, and block abusive customers.
---

The human side of selling lives in a few places in the panel:

- **Refunds**: refund an order from its page under **Transactions**; past
  refunds are on the **Refunds** tab there. See
  [Refunds & disputes](/creators/getting-paid/refunds-and-disputes).
- **Inquiries**: disputes (chargebacks) opened by buyers' banks, where you submit
  evidence before the deadline.
- **Blocked customers**: under **Customers**, block a player who abuses refunds
  or breaks your terms, and unblock them later if needed. A blocked player cannot
  check out on your store.

Keeping this tidy protects your dispute rate. A €15 dispute fee reduces the
purchase price only when the supplier is at fault.
