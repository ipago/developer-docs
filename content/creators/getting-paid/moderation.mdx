---
title: Moderation
description: Handle refund requests and inquiries, and block abusive customers.
---

The **Moderation** area is where you manage the human side of selling.

- **Refunds & inquiries**: review and act on buyer requests in one place.
- **Blocked customers**: block a buyer who abuses refunds or violates your terms,
  and unblock them later if needed.

Keeping this tidy protects your dispute rate, which keeps your payouts smooth and
your account healthy.
