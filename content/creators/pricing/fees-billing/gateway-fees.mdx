---
title: Gateway fees
description: Processing fees charged by payment providers.
---

Payment processing fees are passed through at cost from the payment provider,
on the amount charged to the buyer, VAT included.

- **Cards:** 2.9% + €0.30.
- **Other methods** (PayPal, Klarna, BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL, Bancontact, Link)
  have their own rates.

The **Platform fee** card under **Store settings** in the panel shows the fee for
each payment method your store offers, with a worked example.
