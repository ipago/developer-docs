---
title: Card costs
description: The card cost iPago deducts from each payment, next to the margin.
---

Taking a payment costs money, and that cost is deducted from what you receive.
It is one rate for every payment method (cards, PayPal, Klarna, BLIK,
Przelewy24, iDEAL, Bancontact, Link):

- **2.9% of what the player paid, VAT included,**
- **plus €0.30 per payment.**

The percentage applies to the amount the player was charged, VAT included,
because that is the amount the payment moved. It is taken once per payment, not
per item in the basket. A different rate can be agreed for a store, and the
merchant panel shows the rate your store is on under **Settings**.

The card cost comes on top of the 5% margin. The worked example is on
[Margin & card cost](/creators/pricing/fees-billing).
