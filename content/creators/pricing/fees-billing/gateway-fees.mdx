---
title: Card costs
description: Card and method costs are not deducted from the purchase price.
---

The purchase price is the suggested retail price minus iPago's margin. Costs of
cards and other methods (PayPal, Klarna, BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL, Bancontact,
Link) are **not** deducted from the purchase price, and they are not added on
top of the margin.

iPago may sell at any price and still owes the purchase price. Which method the
player uses does not change that amount.

The margin, the worked example, and the weekly invoice are on
[Margin & purchase price](/creators/pricing/fees-billing).
