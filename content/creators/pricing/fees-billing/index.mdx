A clear breakdown of what 2Pay charges, and when.

## Platform fee

As your platform, 2Pay charges a flat **platform fee of 5%** of the transaction
value. It's the same across every game and product, with no setup costs and no
hidden surprises.

## Gateway fees

Separate from our platform fee, payment providers (gateways) such as **PayPal**,
**Klarna**, and others charge their own processing fees. These vary by provider
and follow each provider's own pricing.

See the full breakdown on the [Gateway fees](/creators/pricing/fees-billing/gateway-fees)
page.

## Chargebacks & payment disputes

Most transactions are covered by our **Seller Protection Program**. When a
transaction is covered:

- You don't pay a chargeback fee.
- You keep your revenue, even if the dispute is lost.
- 2Pay manages the dispute on your behalf.

If a transaction **isn't** covered by the program, it's flagged as such, and:

- A **$20 chargeback fee** applies when the chargeback is handled.
- If the dispute is lost, the amount is deducted from your account.

### Refunds

When you refund a sale, the full original transaction amount is returned to the
buyer, and the gateway fee applies again on the refund. Gateway fees already paid
are not recovered.

For example, on a **$10** sale with a **$0.20** gateway fee: refunding it returns
the full $10 to the buyer, the original $0.20 gateway fee isn't returned, and a
further $0.20 gateway fee applies to the refund itself.

## Payouts

Payout fees depend on your chosen **payout method**, **currency**, and the
**destination country**.

- A **2% exchange fee** applies when you withdraw in a currency different from your
  wallet's currency.
- Rejected or returned payouts still incur this fee. If incorrect details cause a
  payout to bounce, the fee is not refunded.
