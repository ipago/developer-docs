What iPago owes you for each licence, and when it is paid. iPago Sp. z o.o. is
the seller to the player. You are the supplier. There is no contract between you
and the player.

## Margin

iPago's **margin is 5%** of the suggested retail price (RRP) by default. The rate
is stored per store, so a different rate can be agreed.
[Talk to us](mailto:support@ipago.io) for a high-volume store.

## Purchase price

**Purchase price = RRP − margin.**

The RRP is the price you set on the product. The purchase price does not depend
on the price the player actually pays. iPago may sell at any price, including a
discount or a higher price, and still owes the purchase price.

Player VAT is not part of the purchase price. Whether your store shows prices
inclusive or exclusive of VAT only changes the price the player sees. iPago
remits VAT on the sale to the player either way.

## A worked example

RRP of €10.00, margin 5%:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Suggested retail price (RRP) | €10.00 |
| Margin (5% of €10.00) | €0.50 |
| **Purchase price** | **€9.50** |

If iPago charges the player a different amount, the purchase price stays €9.50.
Card costs are not a further deduction.

## Refunds

iPago refunds the player. A refund does not, by itself, change the purchase
price, and there is no separate refund fee. See
[Refunds & disputes](/creators/getting-paid/refunds-and-disputes).

## Chargebacks

iPago handles chargebacks with the player. A **€15** dispute fee reduces the
purchase price **only when the supplier is at fault**. If the supplier is not at
fault, the purchase price is not reduced by the dispute fee.

## Weekly invoice

Purchase prices are collected on a **weekly self-billing purchase invoice**
under article 106d of the Polish VAT Act. The invoice states the **payment
term**. iPago pays it by the due date (**invoice payment**).

That replaces a hold after the sale and a payout you request.
Details are on [Invoice payment](/creators/getting-paid/invoices).

Any currency conversion or intermediary fee charged by the receiving bank is set
by that bank, not by iPago.
