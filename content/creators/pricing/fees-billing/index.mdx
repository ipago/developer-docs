---
title: Fee & Billing Overview
description: How fees and billing work on 2Pay.
---

A clear breakdown of what 2Pay charges, and when. Every fee is taken from the
sale before it reaches your balance; there is nothing to pay separately.

## Platform fee

2Pay charges a **platform fee of 5%** of the sale, excluding VAT. It is taken on
what you earn, not on the tax the buyer paid. The rate is stored per store, so a
different rate can be agreed.

## Payment processing (gateway) fees

The payment provider's processing fee is passed through at cost. For cards it is
**2.9% + €0.30**, computed on the amount actually charged, VAT included, because
that is what the provider charges on. Other methods have their own rates. See
[Gateway fees](/creators/pricing/fees-billing/gateway-fees).

## A worked example

A €10.00 sale to a buyer whose VAT rate is 23%:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Sale, excluding VAT | €10.00 |
| VAT (remitted by 2Pay) | €2.30 |
| Charged to the buyer | €12.30 |
| Platform fee (5% of €10.00) | -€0.50 |
| Card processing (2.9% of €12.30 + €0.30) | -€0.66 |
| **Credited to your balance** | **€8.84** |

## Refunds

When you refund a sale, the buyer gets back everything they paid, and your
balance is reduced by the sale amount excluding VAT. The platform fee and the
processing fee on the original sale are not returned. No extra fee is charged for
the refund itself. See [Refunds & disputes](/creators/getting-paid/refunds-and-disputes).

## Chargebacks & payment disputes

2Pay handles disputes as the Merchant of Record. Each dispute carries a
**€15.00** fee from the card networks, win or lose. If the dispute is lost, the
sale is reversed from your balance the same way a full refund is.

## Payouts

Payouts to your bank account are free. Payouts are normally made in EUR; any
currency conversion or intermediary fees are set by the receiving bank, not by
2Pay.
