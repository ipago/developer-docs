What iPago owes you for each licence, and when it is paid. iPago Sp. z o.o. is
the seller to the player. You are the supplier. There is no contract between you
and the player.

## Margin

iPago's **margin is 5%** of the price the player paid before VAT, by default.
The rate is stored per store, so a different rate can be agreed.
[Talk to us](mailto:support@ipago.io) for a high-volume store.

## Card cost

Each payment also carries the **card cost: 2.9% of what the player paid, VAT
included, plus €0.30**. It is the same for every payment method. See
[Card costs](/creators/pricing/fees-billing/gateway-fees).

## What you receive

**You receive = price before VAT − margin − card cost.**

The price is what the player actually paid before VAT, after any sale, coupon or
gift card. Player VAT is not part of it: iPago is the seller and remits that VAT.
Whether your store shows prices inclusive or exclusive of VAT only changes the
price the player sees.

## A worked example

A €10.00 item sold to a buyer in Poland (23% VAT), on a store with the default
rates:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Price before VAT | €10.00 |
| VAT (23%, kept by iPago) | €2.30 |
| Player pays | €12.30 |
| Margin (5% of €10.00) | −€0.50 |
| Card cost (2.9% of €12.30 = €0.36, plus €0.30) | −€0.66 |
| **You receive** | **€8.84** |

A buyer outside the EU pays no VAT, so the card cost is 2.9% of €10.00 plus
€0.30, which is €0.59, and you receive €8.91.

## Refunds

iPago refunds the player. There is no separate refund fee. See
[Refunds & disputes](/creators/getting-paid/refunds-and-disputes).

## Chargebacks

iPago handles chargebacks with the player. A **€15** dispute fee reduces the
purchase price **only when the supplier is at fault**. If the supplier is not at
fault, the purchase price is not reduced by the dispute fee.

## Weekly invoice

Purchase prices are collected on a **weekly self-billing purchase invoice**
under article 106d of the Polish VAT Act. The invoice states the **payment
term**. iPago pays it by the due date (**invoice payment**).

That replaces a hold after the sale and a payout you request.
Details are on [Invoice payment](/creators/getting-paid/invoices).

Any currency conversion or intermediary fee charged by the receiving bank is set
by that bank, not by iPago.
