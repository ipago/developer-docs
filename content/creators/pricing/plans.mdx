## Free trial

New to **Creator Plus**? Try it free for **30 days**. You get full access to every
Creator Plus feature during the trial, with no commitment, and if you don't
upgrade, your store simply moves back to the free Starter plan.

## Plans & features

2Pay comes in two plans, so you can start for free and upgrade only when you're
ready to scale.

### Free

The **Free** plan includes everything you need to start selling: a hosted
storefront, 100+ payment methods, packages and subscriptions, and automatic
in-game delivery.

### Creator Plus

**Creator Plus** is the paid plan. On top of everything in Free, it unlocks the
advanced tools to grow: scaling and automation, full branding control, and deeper
sales analytics.
