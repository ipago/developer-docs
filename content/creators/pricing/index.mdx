iPago Sp. z o.o. buys licences from you (the supplier) and resells them to
players in its own name. There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and no per-store
fee. Every store gets the same features.

## What you are paid

- **Margin: 5%** of the price the player paid before VAT, by default. The rate
  is stored per store and can be agreed differently.
- **Card cost: 2.9% + €0.30** per payment, on what the player paid, VAT
  included. The same for every payment method.
- **You receive** the price before VAT minus the margin and the card cost.

VAT on the sale to the player is iPago's, because iPago is the seller, and it is
not deducted from what you receive.

For the calculation and a worked example, see
[Margin & card cost](/creators/pricing/fees-billing). What is included is on
the [Plans](/creators/pricing/plans) page.
