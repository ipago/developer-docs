2Pay has plans that scale with you. Start for free, and upgrade only when you
need more.

## Our plans

### Starter

The **Starter** plan is **free**. It gives you everything you need to set up your
store and start selling.

### Creator Plus

**Creator Plus** is our premium plan. It unlocks extra features as you grow, such
as **branding control** and **higher limits**, so you can scale your
monetization.

For a full breakdown of what each plan includes, see the
[Starter &amp; Creator Plus Plan](/creators/pricing/plans) page.

## Fees Overview

On top of your plan, 2Pay charges a simple **5% platform fee** on each sale. No
setup costs and no hidden surprises, you always know what you keep.

For exactly how fees are calculated, applied, and billed, see the
[Fee &amp; Billing Overview](/creators/pricing/fees-billing).
