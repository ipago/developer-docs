iPago Sp. z o.o. buys licences from you (the supplier) and resells them to
players in its own name. There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and no per-store
fee. Every store gets the same features.

## What you are paid

- **Margin: 5%** of the suggested retail price (RRP), by default. This is
  iPago's margin. The rate is stored per store and can be agreed differently.
- **Purchase price** = RRP minus the margin. iPago owes this amount.
- **Resale price.** iPago may sell at any price. It still owes the purchase price.
- **Dispute fee: €15** reduces the purchase price only when the supplier is at
  fault. It is not taken on every dispute.

Card and method costs are not deducted from the purchase price. Invoice payment
has no extra fee. VAT on the sale to the player is iPago's, because iPago is the
seller, and it is not part of the purchase price.

For the calculation, the weekly invoice, and the payment term, see
[Margin & purchase price](/creators/pricing/fees-billing). What is included is on
the [Plans](/creators/pricing/plans) page.
