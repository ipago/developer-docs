2Pay has plans that scale with you. Start for free, and upgrade only when you
need more.

## Our plans

### Starter

The **Starter** plan is **free**. It gives you everything you need to set up your
store and start selling.

### Creator Plus

**Creator Plus** is our premium plan. It unlocks extra features as you grow, such
as **branding control** and **higher limits**, so you can scale your
monetization.
