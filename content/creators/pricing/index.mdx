iPago charges per sale. There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and no per-store
fee, and every store gets the same features.

## Fees overview

- **Platform fee: 5%** of the sale, excluding VAT. This is iPago's own cut.
- **Payment processing**, passed through at cost from the payment provider. For
  cards it is 2.9% + €0.30 of the amount charged, VAT included.
- **Chargebacks: €15.00** per dispute, the card networks' fee.

Payouts are free, and VAT filing and remittance are included.

For how fees are calculated and applied, see the
[Fee &amp; Billing Overview](/creators/pricing/fees-billing). What is and is not
included is on the [Plans](/creators/pricing/plans) page.
