you're a large studio, a Minecraft server, a small developer, or a hobbyist
launching your very first project, 2Pay is built for you.

We're an **all-in-one monetization solution**, designed to grow and scale right
alongside your project.

These docs have everything you need to set up and run your 2Pay store.
