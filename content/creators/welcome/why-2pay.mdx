---
title: Why 2Pay
description: Why creators, studios, and servers choose 2Pay to monetize.
---

Everything you need to sell, scale, and get paid, in one platform.

## Built to start fast

- **Quick, easy store set-up.** Go from sign-up to your first sale without a
  developer.
- **Made for your game.** First-class support for popular games and servers, plus
  fully custom setups.
- **Make it yours.** Customizable storefronts with themes so your store matches
  your brand.

## Sell to anyone, anywhere

- **Global payment coverage.** Accept 100+ payment methods, cards, wallets, and
  local options, so buyers everywhere can pay the way they already do.
- **Tax handled across every country.** As your Merchant of Record, we collect
  and settle sales tax and VAT in the markets you sell to. You never register or
  file in each country, you settle with us, and only with us, in one place.

## Get paid, and stay protected

- **Fast payouts.** Get your money within 7 days, no waiting weeks for your
  earnings.
- **Seller protection.** We take on fraud and chargebacks, shielding you from the
  disputes and risk that come with selling online.

## Grow your revenue

- **Packages, bundles & subscriptions.** Sell one-off items or recurring plans,
  and group them into bundles.
- **Discounts, campaigns & loyalty.** Run sales, promo codes, and loyalty
  programs to bring buyers back.
- **Advanced analytics.** Track sales and performance to see what's working.

## Built for developers and scale

- **Automatic in-game delivery.** Orders are fulfilled in-game automatically, the
  moment they're paid for.
- **Full API access.** A complete, enterprise-friendly API to build whatever you
  need on top of 2Pay.
- **Dedicated support.** Real help when you need it, especially as you grow.
