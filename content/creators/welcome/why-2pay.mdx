---
title: Why 2Pay
description: Why creators, studios, and servers choose 2Pay to monetize.
---

Everything you need to sell, scale, and get paid, in one platform.

## Built to start fast

- **Quick, easy store set-up.** Go from sign-up to your first sale without a
  developer.
- **Made for your game.** Deliver to Minecraft servers through the 2Pay plugin or
  RCON, or to any system through webhooks and the API.
- **Make it yours.** A hosted storefront with three layouts, light or dark
  appearance, your accent colour, and your logo.

## Sell to anyone

- **Local payment methods.** Cards, PayPal, Klarna, BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL,
  Bancontact, and Link, so buyers can pay the way they already do.
- **VAT handled for you.** As your Merchant of Record, we calculate VAT for the
  buyer's country, collect it, and remit it. You don't register or file for it in
  each country.

## Get paid

- **Clear payouts.** Earnings become available after a 7-day hold, and you pay
  them out to your bank account, with no payout fee.
- **Disputes handled.** 2Pay handles chargebacks as the seller, and you supply
  evidence from the panel.

## Grow your revenue

- **Packages & subscriptions.** Sell one-off items or monthly and yearly plans.
- **Sales, coupons & gift cards.** Run discounts and promo codes, and issue gift
  cards.
- **Statistics.** Track revenue, orders, and your top products.

## Built for developers

- **Automatic in-game delivery.** Orders are delivered to your game server when
  they are paid for.
- **API access.** A Developer API and webhooks to build on top of 2Pay.
