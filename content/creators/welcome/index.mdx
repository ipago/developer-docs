iPago is a platform for selling digital content, no matter who you are. Whether
you're a large studio, a Minecraft server, an indie developer, or a hobbyist
launching your very first project, iPago is built to fit.

We're an **all-in-one monetization solution**: storefront, checkout, payments,
tax, and payouts in one place. Start in minutes with sensible defaults, then grow
into deeper customization and your own integrations as your project takes off, no
replatforming and no rebuild.

You bring the products; we handle the hard parts of selling them online, from
collecting payment in your buyer's preferred method, to keeping every sale
compliant, to paying you out.

These docs cover everything you need to set up and run your iPago store, from your
first product to managing a growing catalog.

<Callout type="info">
  Looking for technical implementation and integration guides? Head to the
  [Developer documentation](/dev).
</Callout>

## Before you get started

Many links and actions throughout this documentation point into your iPago
dashboard and need an iPago account. If you don't have one yet, create it first so
you can follow along with the guides.

<Callout title="Create your account">
  [Create your iPago account](https://app.ipago.io) to get started.
