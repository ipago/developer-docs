iPago is a platform for selling digital content, no matter who you are. Whether
you're a large studio, a Minecraft server, an indie developer, or a hobbyist
launching your very first project, iPago is built to fit.

iPago Sp. z o.o. is a **reseller**. It buys licences and other digital content
from you (the supplier) and resells them to players in its own name. There is no
contract between you and the player. You set the price. iPago pays you what the
player paid before VAT, minus its 5% margin and the card cost, on a weekly invoice.

You bring the products. iPago sells them to players, from checkout through
refunds and complaints, and pays the weekly invoice by its due date.

These docs cover everything you need to set up and run your iPago store, from your
first product to managing a growing catalog.
