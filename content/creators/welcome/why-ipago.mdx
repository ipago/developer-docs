---
title: Why iPago
description: Why creators, studios, and servers choose iPago to monetize.
---

Everything you need to sell, scale, and get paid, in one platform.

## Built to start fast

- **Quick, easy store set-up.** Go from sign-up to your first sale without a
  developer.
- **Made for your game.** Deliver to Minecraft servers through the iPago plugin or
  RCON, or to any system through webhooks and the API.
- **Make it yours.** A hosted storefront with three layouts, light or dark
  appearance, your accent colour, and your logo.

## Sell to anyone

- **Local payment methods.** Cards, PayPal, Klarna, BLIK, Przelewy24, iDEAL,
  Bancontact, and Link, so players can pay the way they already do.
- **VAT on the player sale.** iPago Sp. z o.o. is the seller to the player. It
  calculates VAT for the player's country, collects it, and remits it. You do not
  register or file for that sale in each country.

## Invoice payment

- **Two deductions, stated up front.** iPago's margin (5% of the price before
  VAT by default, negotiable per store) and the card cost (2.9% of what the
  player paid plus €0.30 per payment). Nothing else comes off.
- **Weekly invoices.** iPago issues a self-billing purchase invoice each week and
  pays it by the due date on the invoice. There is no hold and no payout to
  request.
- **Disputes handled.** iPago handles refunds, complaints, and chargebacks with
  the player. A €15 dispute fee reduces the purchase price only when the supplier
  is at fault.

## Grow your revenue

- **Packages & subscriptions.** Sell one-off items or monthly and yearly plans.
- **Sales, coupons & gift cards.** Run discounts and promo codes, and issue gift
  cards.
- **Statistics.** Track orders and your top products.

## Built for developers

- **Automatic in-game delivery.** Orders are delivered to your game server when
  they are paid for.
- **API access.** A Developer API and webhooks to build on top of iPago.
