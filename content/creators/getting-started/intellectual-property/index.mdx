Selling on 2Pay means only listing content you're actually allowed to sell. This
page explains what intellectual property is, what you can and can't list, and how
to check your own content before it goes live.

We enforce these rules in line with our
[Acceptable Use Policy](https://2pay.gg/terms/acceptable-use-policy), and you
should also respect the license terms of whatever game or platform your content is
for.

## What is intellectual property?

**Intellectual property (IP)** is original work, such as names, designs, artwork,
and other content, that legally belongs to a person or company. It's usually
protected by **copyright**, **trademarks**, or **patents**, which means others
can't use, sell, or distribute it without permission.

Commonly protected IP includes:

- **Brand names**, unique company or product names.
- **Logos and design elements**, emblems, stylized fonts, and distinctive symbols.
- **Character designs**, recognizable figures from games, comics, or animations.
- **Product shapes**, iconic silhouettes (cars, weapons, gadgets) tied to real
  brands.

## What you can't sell

You may not list or distribute content that includes:

- **Real brand references**, actual names, logos, slogans, or imagery.
- **Altered brand names**, misspellings or sound-alikes meant to imitate a brand.
- **Cloned character models**, replicas or rips from commercial games.
- **Lookalike products**, vehicles, weapons, or items that clearly resemble
  real-world models, even with the logos removed.

<Callout type="warn">
  Intent matters. Making small changes to someone else's copyrighted work does not
  make it compliant.
</Callout>

## What you can sell

You're welcome to list and sell:

- **Original content**, fully custom models, textures, names, and styles.
- **Lore-friendly designs**, fictional creations that fit your game's world.
- **Stylized assets**, generic items inspired by a broad category rather than a
  specific real-world brand.

Creativity is encouraged, as long as it stays inside legal boundaries.

## Vehicle designs: a common risk

Vehicles are one of the most frequent sources of accidental infringement. To stay
safe:

- Remove all **badges, emblems, and logos**.
- Avoid copying recognizable **shapes and silhouettes**.
- Prefer **fictional or heavily modified** designs.

To check a model, run a **reverse image search** (for example with Google Lens).
If it closely matches a real-world brand or product, it's most likely not
compliant.

## Disclosure requirements

If a package includes **customizable or user-selectable content**, you must
clearly show that content on your store page. Make sure buyers can:

- see **item names, previews, or thumbnails**,
- view **images, videos, or links** of the items,
- understand exactly what they're buying **before** they join a server or complete
  checkout.

Being transparent builds trust and protects both you and your customers.

## IP compliance checklist

Before you list something for sale, confirm:

| Check | Confirmed |
| --- | :---: |
| Content is original, or clearly fictional and lore-friendly | ☐ |
| No real-world brands, logos, or names | ☐ |
| No altered or misspelled brand names | ☐ |
| Models and items don't resemble real products | ☐ |
| Logos and brand badges removed from all assets | ☐ |
| A reverse image search shows no likeness to real items | ☐ |
| Customizable items are disclosed on the store page | ☐ |
| Buyers can preview items before joining or paying | ☐ |

<Callout type="info">
  Not sure about a specific item? Leave it off your store until you've confirmed
  it's original or properly licensed. Repeated or serious infringement can lead to
  content removal and account action under our policies.
</Callout>
