---
title: Accounts & team
description: How your login, accounts, stores, and team roles fit together on iPago.
---

iPago is built around three things: your **login**, the **account** you sell
under, and the **stores** inside it. This page explains how they fit, and how to
bring teammates in.

## Your login

Your login is you, a single person, with your own email and password. Keep it to
yourself: to work with others, add them to your account with their own login
instead of sharing yours.

Under **Account** in the panel you can:

- **Confirm your email address.** iPago sends a confirmation link when you sign
  up. You must confirm it before you can submit a store for review.
- **Turn on two-factor authentication.** Signing in then asks for a code from
  your authenticator app after your password.
- **Save your recovery codes.** When you turn on two-factor authentication you get
  10 one-time recovery codes, shown once. Each one signs you in if you lose your
  phone. You can generate a new set at any time, which replaces the old one.
- Change your profile and password.

## Accounts

An **account** is the seller: you as an individual, or your business. It holds
what belongs to the seller as a whole:

- **identity verification** (KYC), done once for the account,
- the **bank account** payouts are sent to,
- the **team**: the people who can work on it,
- one or more **stores**.

## Stores

A **store** is a single selling operation, for example one game server or one
game. Each store has its own:

- catalog of products, categories, sales, coupons and gift cards,
- currency, webstore URL, branding and settings,
- balance, orders, refunds and payouts,
- API keys, webhooks and game server connections.

If you run several game servers, give each its own store under the same account.
Their catalogs and revenue stay separate.

## Team roles

Owners and admins can add people to the account under **Store settings**. The
person must already have an iPago login; you add them by email. Every member has
one role for the whole account:

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including changing the payout bank account. |
| **Admin** | Manage stores, catalog, webstore, refunds, payouts, API keys and the team. Sees the bank account masked. |
| **Member** | View the panel, without changing settings or moving money. |

<Callout type="info">
  Add teammates instead of sharing your login. Everyone keeps their own login,
  and you can change their role or remove them at any time.
</Callout>
