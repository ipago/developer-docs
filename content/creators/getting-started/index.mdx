Welcome to **2Pay**. Getting set up is quick, creating an account takes only a
few minutes.

Whether you're someone who wants to start monetizing your game server, or a
studio looking to integrate payments into your game world, you're in the right
place. This is where you'll learn everything you need to get started.

<Callout title="Create your account">
  [Create your 2Pay account](https://app.2pay.gg) to follow along.
</Callout>

## Our agreements

Before you start selling, it's worth knowing the terms you're agreeing to. The
**Creator Agreement** is the main one, it's the core contract between you and
2Pay. The others cover privacy, acceptable use, and compliance. You can read all
of them in full on our website.
