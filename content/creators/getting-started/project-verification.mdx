title: Project verification

---

Before a project can go live, it goes through **verification**. Once you've
created your project and marked it as ready to launch, our team reviews it to make
sure everything is correct and meets our guidelines.

## What we check

- **No IP infringement.** Your products must not infringe anyone's intellectual
  property.
- **The game must be open.** The game your project sells for needs to be publicly
  available and live.
- **Everything works as described.** Products do what their descriptions say and
  deliver correctly.

## The project setup checklist

Everything you need to do to reach review is available in your panel as a
**Project setup** checklist. Work through it step by step and track your progress
as you go. When every item is complete, submit your project for review.

## Tips for a successful review

To get approved quickly, make sure that:

- **All products are complete, functional, and properly described.** Buyers should
  know exactly what they're getting.
- **Every test and placeholder product or piece of content is removed.** Nothing
  labelled "test", "example", or filled with placeholder text should remain.
- **Nothing violates our intellectual property guidelines.** Only sell content you
  own or are authorized to sell, see the
  [Acceptable Use Policy](https://2pay.gg/terms/acceptable-use-policy) for details.

<Callout type="info">
  Run through the Project setup checklist in your panel one more time before
  submitting, it's the fastest path to approval.
</Callout>
