---
title: Store review
description: How store review works and how to pass it.
---

Before a store can take real payments, it goes through **review**. Once you've
built your store and submitted it from the **Webstore** page, the iPago team
checks that everything is correct and meets our guidelines.

## Before you can submit

The panel only lets you submit once:

- your **email address is confirmed**,
- your **account is verified** (see [Identity verification](/creators/getting-started/identity-verification)),
- the store has a **URL**,
- the store has at least one **active live-mode product**.

The setup checklist on your store's dashboard tracks these steps. The full list,
including what is worth doing beyond the minimum, is in
[Going live](/creators/your-store/going-live).

## What we check

- **No IP infringement.** Your products must not infringe anyone's intellectual
  property.
- **The game must be open.** The game your store sells for needs to be publicly
  available and live.
- **Everything works as described.** Products do what their descriptions say and
  deliver correctly.

## Tips for a successful review

To get approved quickly, make sure that:

- **All products are complete, functional, and properly described.** Buyers should
  know exactly what they're getting.
- **No test or placeholder products remain in live mode.** Nothing labelled
  "test", "example", or filled with placeholder text should be visible to buyers.
  Keep test products in test mode.
- **Nothing violates our intellectual property guidelines.** Only sell content you
  own or are authorized to sell, see
  [Intellectual property](/creators/getting-started/intellectual-property) for
  what's allowed.

You get an email with the result. If the store is rejected, the email and the
Webstore page give the reason; fix it and submit again.
