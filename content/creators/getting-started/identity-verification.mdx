To keep the platform safe and stay compliant with regulations and the
requirements and standards of global payment providers, we verify every seller's
identity **before their store is approved**.

## Why verification is required

- **Platform integrity.** Real, verified sellers keep 2Pay trustworthy for
  buyers and creators alike.
- **Compliance.** Identity checks (KYC) are required by law and by our payment
  partners to prevent money laundering and fraud.
- **One account per person.** Verification confirms that each account belongs to a
  single real person.

## Age requirements

You must be at least **18 years old** to open a store on 2Pay.

If you're under 18, you can't open a store yourself. A **parent or legal
guardian** must create and run the store on your behalf, and complete verification
in their own name.

## Accepted documents

You can verify with any of the following:

- **Passport**
- **Driver's license**
- **National ID card**

Make sure the document is valid, in date, and that photos are clear and
uncropped.

## The verification process

We use **Onfido**, a trusted identity-verification provider. The process is quick
and happens right in your panel:

1. **Start verification** from your panel when prompted.
2. **Choose your document type**, passport, driver's license, or national ID.
3. **Capture or upload** clear photos of the document.
4. **Take a live selfie** so Onfido can confirm you match the document (a quick
   liveness check).
5. **Submit.** Onfido runs its automated checks.
6. **Get your result**, usually within a few minutes.

## Is my personal data safe?

Yes. Verification is handled by our partner **Onfido**. Your details and documents
are processed securely and used only for identity verification and compliance, in
line with our [Privacy Policy](https://2pay.gg/terms/privacy-policy). We never see
your full document data outside of this regulated process.

## FAQ

### I'm under 18, can I open a store?

No. You must be 18 or older. In that case, a parent or legal guardian can open and
run the store instead, and verify in their own name.

### How long does verification take?

Usually just a few minutes. Occasionally Onfido may need a little longer to run
extra checks, in which case you'll be notified.

### Can I verify myself on more than one account?

No. We allow only **one account per person**. During verification we'll detect
that you already have an account, and a second verification won't be accepted.
