To keep the platform safe and stay compliant with regulations and the
requirements of our payment partners, we verify every seller (KYC) **before
their store is approved**. Verification belongs to your account, so it covers
all of the account's stores.

## Why verification is required

- **Platform integrity.** Real, verified sellers keep iPago trustworthy for
  buyers and creators alike.
- **Compliance.** Identity checks are required by law and by our payment
  partners to prevent money laundering and fraud.
- **Getting paid.** Payouts need a verified account.

## Age requirements

You must be at least **18 years old** to open a store on iPago.

If you're under 18, you can't open a store yourself. A **parent or legal
guardian** must create and run the store on your behalf, and complete verification
in their own name.

## The verification process

Verification happens in your panel and is reviewed by the iPago team.

1. **Start verification** from the banner in your dashboard.
2. **Choose individual or business.**
3. **Enter your details.**
   - As an individual: legal name, date of birth, country, and address.
   - As a business: legal entity name, business structure, tax or registration
     ID, country, registered address, the representative's name, and the
     beneficial owners.
4. **Upload an identity document**, such as a passport, driver's license, or
   national ID card, as a PDF, PNG, or JPEG. Make sure it is valid, in date, and
   clear and uncropped.
5. **Review and submit.**

The review typically takes **1 to 3 business days**. You get an email when your
account is approved, or when we need more information, together with the reason.

## Is my personal data safe?

Your details and documents are stored securely, kept apart from your store data,
and used only for identity verification and compliance, in line with our
[Privacy Policy](https://ipago.io/terms/privacy-policy).

## FAQ

### I'm under 18, can I open a store?

No. You must be 18 or older. In that case, a parent or legal guardian can open and
run the store instead, and verify in their own name.

### How long does verification take?

Typically 1 to 3 business days.

### What if my verification is not accepted?

You get an email with the reason. Fix what is asked and submit again from the
same page.
