We want 2Pay to be a **safe, inclusive, and professional** environment for
everyone who builds with us, both individual creators and game studios.

As the **Merchant of Record**, 2Pay is legally responsible for the content sold
through our platform. Everything you list has to comply with our terms, applicable
policies, and industry regulations, so we can keep the platform safe and
trustworthy for all sides. For that reason, **adult content is strictly
prohibited on 2Pay**.

This guide explains why these rules exist, what we consider adult content, and how
to keep your stores compliant. It works alongside our
[Acceptable Use Policy](https://2pay.gg/terms/acceptable-use-policy).

## Why these guidelines exist

- **Protect users, especially minors,** from explicit or inappropriate material.
- **Stay compliant** with our payment partners and with international law.
- **Keep a professional experience** for creators, studios, and buyers alike.

## What counts as adult content

Adult content is anything **Not Safe For Work (NSFW)** or unsuitable for minors
and general audiences. This includes, but isn't limited to:

### Extreme violence or gore

- Graphic dismemberment, torture, or excessive blood.
- Realistic depictions that glorify violence.

### Nudity

- Exposed sensitive areas, regardless of a character's species or how realistic
  it is.
- Censored or pixelated nudity.
- Sheer or transparent clothing that reveals sensitive areas.

### Sexualized content

- Sexual acts or suggestive poses.
- Items tied to fetish or sexual behavior.
- Names, descriptions, or visuals that imply sexual activity.

### Profanity and hate

- Excessive swearing or vulgarity in names, descriptions, or images.
- Slurs or language targeting protected groups.

<Callout type="warn">
  We review images as a whole. Insufficient clothing, semi-transparent outfits
  that draw attention to sensitive areas, or suggestive poses can make content
  inappropriate even when nothing is fully exposed. If in doubt, leave it out.
</Callout>

## What's allowed

Creative freedom is welcome, within safe and professional boundaries. For example:

- Lore-friendly cosmetics, costumes, and skins that are fully clothed.
- Horror or action themes that avoid realistic gore or dismemberment.
- Language that fits your community's tone, as long as it doesn't break our
  profanity or harassment rules.

Studios working on mature titles must still keep **storefront content**
(descriptions, thumbnails, banners, and previews) suitable for general audiences,
even if the in-game content itself carries a mature rating.

## Your responsibilities

As a 2Pay creator or studio, you're responsible for:

- making sure everything you upload follows these guidelines,
- removing or editing any content we flag, when we notify you,
- moderating user-submitted material where it applies (uploads, screenshots, and
  so on).

Not complying can lead to:

- removal of individual items or packages,
- temporary or permanent suspension of your store or account,
- delays in store approval or payouts.

## How to stay compliant

- Avoid real-world adult brands, adult products, or explicit references.
- Make sure character models and avatars are appropriately dressed.
- Double-check your **store banners**, **product thumbnails**, and **video
  previews**, not just the items themselves.
- Use safe-search and content filters to sanity-check your images.

<Callout type="info">
  Not sure whether something is allowed? Check the
  [Acceptable Use Policy](https://2pay.gg/terms/acceptable-use-policy), or reach
  out to our team at support@2pay.gg before you list it.
</Callout>
