---
title: Store settings
description: Identity, tax display, and payment methods for your store.
---

Everything about how your store looks and charges lives under **Settings**.

## Identity

Set your display name, description, accent color, logo, and store URL. If you sell
in-game items, you can require a **player identifier** at checkout and choose its
label (for example "Minecraft username").

## Tax display

Choose whether your prices are shown **exclusive** of tax (tax added at checkout)
or **inclusive** (tax already in the price). 2Pay remits the tax either way as
your Merchant of Record; this setting only changes the sticker price buyers see.

## Payment methods

By default your checkout offers the methods most likely to convert for each
buyer's country. You can narrow this to a specific set (for example cards and BLIK)
from settings.

<Callout type="info">
  A payment method only appears if it is enabled for your store and available for
  the buyer's country and currency.
</Callout>
