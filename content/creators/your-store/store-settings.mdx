title: Store settings
description: Identity, tax display, and payment methods for your store.
---

How your store looks is set under **Webstore**; how it charges is set under
**Store settings**. Owners and admins can change both.

## Identity

Under **Webstore**, set your store URL, display name, description, accent colour,
theme, light or dark appearance, and logo. If you sell in-game items, you can
require a **player identifier** at checkout and choose its label (for example
"Minecraft username"). The full list, with limits, is in
[Webstore customization](/dev/webstores/customization).

Under **Store settings**, set your **business name** and **support email**,
both shown in your storefront's footer.

## Tax display

Choose whether your prices are shown **exclusive** of tax (VAT added at checkout)
or **inclusive** (VAT already in the price). iPago remits the tax either way as
your Merchant of Record; this setting only changes the sticker price buyers see.

## Payment methods

You can limit your checkout to a chosen set of methods: Card, BLIK, Przelewy24,
Klarna, PayPal, iDEAL, Bancontact, and Link. With no selection, the payment
provider's default set applies.

<Callout type="info">
  A payment method only appears if it is enabled for your store and available for
  the buyer's country and currency.
</Callout>

## Team

Under **Store settings**, owners and admins can add people who already have a
iPago login to the account, as an **admin** or a **member**. See
[Accounts & team](/creators/getting-started/account-structure).
