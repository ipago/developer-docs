---
title: Subscriptions
description: Sell recurring memberships and perks that renew automatically.
---

A **subscription** is a product that bills on a schedule (monthly or yearly)
instead of once. Use it for memberships, VIP perks, or anything with ongoing
access.

- Buyers pay the first invoice at checkout, then 2Pay bills each renewal
  automatically.
- When a renewal succeeds, access continues. If a subscription is canceled,
  access ends at the period boundary.
- You can see active subscriptions for a store in the panel, and cancel one when
  needed.

<Callout type="info">
  As with one-off sales, tax and payment collection on every renewal are handled
  for you. You just keep delivering the perk.
</Callout>
