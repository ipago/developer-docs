---
title: Subscriptions
description: Sell recurring memberships and perks that renew automatically.
---

A **subscription** is a product that bills on a schedule (monthly or yearly)
instead of once. Use it for memberships, VIP perks, or anything with ongoing
access. Create one like any other product and set its type to **Recurring
subscription**.

- Buyers subscribe one at a time from your storefront; a subscription is not
  added to the basket. They pay the first period at checkout, then iPago bills each
  renewal automatically.
- When a renewal succeeds, access continues.
- You can see a store's subscriptions under **Subscriptions** in the sidebar,
  and cancel one there. A canceled subscription does not
  renew; it ends at the close of the period already paid for.

<Callout type="info">
  As with one-off sales, iPago bills the player and remits VAT on every renewal.
  The purchase price is still the suggested retail price minus the margin. If you
  deliver through webhooks, react to `subscription.renewed` and
  `subscription.canceled` to keep access in step. When a renewal is declined the
  plan shows as past due, the player is emailed a link to pay with another card
  (when their email is on file),
  and the charge is retried. Nothing is delivered for that period until it is paid.
</Callout>
