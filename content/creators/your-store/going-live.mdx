---
title: Going live checklist
description: What to finish before you submit your store for review.
---

Before buyers can pay, your store is reviewed. Work through this checklist, then
submit for review from the panel.

- **Verify your identity and project.** See [Getting verified](/creators/getting-started).
- **Add products** with clear names, prices, and delivery configured.
- **Organize categories** so the store reads well.
- **Set your store identity**: name, logo, accent color, and description.
- **Choose your tax display** (inclusive or exclusive) and payment methods.
- **Add payout details** so you can be paid.

When everything checks out, submit for review. Once approved, your store goes live
at its storefront URL and starts accepting payments.
