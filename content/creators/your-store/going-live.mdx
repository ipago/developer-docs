---
title: Going live checklist
description: What to finish before you submit your store for review.
---

Before buyers can pay, your store is reviewed by iPago. The panel only lets you
submit once these are done, and the review request is refused without them:

- **Confirm your email address** from the link iPago sends you.
- **Verify your account.** See [Identity verification](/creators/getting-started/identity-verification).
- **Choose your store URL** under **Webstore**.
- **Add at least one active live-mode product.**

Also worth doing before you submit, so the review goes smoothly:

- **Organize categories** so the store reads well.
- **Set your store identity**: display name, logo, accent colour, and description.
- **Choose your tax display** (inclusive or exclusive) and payment methods.
- **Add your bank account** under **Balances**, **Payouts**, so you can be paid
  once sales come in. See [Payouts](/creators/getting-paid/payouts).

## Try a test order

On the **Webstore** page, **Place a test order** opens your storefront in test
mode: it shows your test-mode products and takes test payments, so you can run a
purchase end to end without real money. It works before and after approval.
**Preview storefront** shows the store as buyers will see it.

## Submit

Submit for review from the **Webstore** page. You get an email when iPago approves
or rejects the store; a rejection comes with a reason, and you can fix it and
submit again. Once approved, your store goes live at `{slug}.ipago.io` and starts
accepting payments. See [Store review](/creators/getting-started/project-verification)
for what iPago checks.
