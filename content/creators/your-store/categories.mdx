---
title: Categories
description: Organize your catalog so buyers find what they want fast.
---

**Categories** group your products into sections on your storefront (for example
"Ranks", "Crates", "Currency"). A clear structure lifts conversion, because buyers
reach the right product in fewer clicks.

- Create categories under **Stock**, then assign products to them.
- Reorder categories to control how your storefront reads top to bottom.
- Deleting a category does not delete its products; they simply become
  uncategorized until you reassign them.
