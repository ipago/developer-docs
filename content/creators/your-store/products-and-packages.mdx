---
title: Adding products & packages
description: Create the things you sell and describe how they are delivered.
---

A **product** (also called a package) is one thing a buyer can purchase: a rank, a
currency bundle, a cosmetic, a key, or a subscription. You create products in the
panel under **Stock**.

## The essentials

- **Name and description**: what the buyer is getting.
- **Price**: in your store's currency. Tax is handled for you as Merchant of
  Record, so you set the price you want to earn from.
- **Category**: where it appears in your store (see [Categories](/creators/your-store/categories)).

## Delivery

Each product describes **how it is delivered** after payment. Depending on your
setup that can be a message with instructions, a code or key, or an automated
delivery to your game or server. You configure delivery per product so that the
moment a payment is confirmed, the buyer gets exactly what they bought.

<Callout type="info">
  Prices inherit your store's currency, so you never set currency per product. To
  sell in another currency, create another store in that currency.
</Callout>
