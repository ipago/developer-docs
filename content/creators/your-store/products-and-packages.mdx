---
title: Adding products & packages
description: Create the things you sell and describe how they are delivered.
---

A **product** (also called a package) is one thing a buyer can purchase: a rank, a
currency bundle, a cosmetic, a key, or a subscription. You create products in the
panel under **Product Catalog**, then **Products**.

## The essentials

- **Name and description**: what the buyer is getting.
- **Price**: the suggested retail price, in your store's currency. The purchase
  price is that RRP minus the margin. Whether the price you enter includes VAT
  depends on your [tax display](/creators/your-store/store-settings#tax-display)
  setting. iPago remits VAT on the sale to the player.
- **Category**: where it appears in your store (see [Categories](/creators/your-store/categories)).
- **Type**: a one-time purchase, or a recurring
  [subscription](/creators/your-store/subscriptions) billed monthly or yearly.
- **Image** and **active**: an inactive product is hidden from your storefront.

## Delivery

Each product says **how it is delivered** after payment:

- **Game server**: run commands on your game server when the order is paid,
  through the iPago plugin or RCON. See the [Game API](/dev/game-api/overview).
- **Webhook**: your own system receives the order through a
  [webhook](/dev/webhooks/overview) and delivers it.
- **Manual**: you fulfil the order yourself.
- **No delivery**: an informational package.

<Callout type="info">
  Prices inherit your store's currency, so you never set currency per product. To
  sell in another currency, create another store in that currency.
</Callout>

## Test-mode products

Products live in either **live** or **test** mode, switched at the top of the
panel. Test-mode products are what you buy when you
[place a test order](/creators/your-store/going-live#try-a-test-order). Buyers
only ever see live-mode products.
