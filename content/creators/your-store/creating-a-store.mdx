title: Creating your store
description: Spin up your first 2Pay store and give it an identity buyers recognize.
---

Your **store** is where you sell. One account can hold several stores, each with
its own catalog, currency, branding, and settings.

## Create a store

From your dashboard, choose **Create your first store** (or create another one
from the store picker). You set:

- A **name** for the store.
- A **currency**, the currency your prices are listed in. Every product in the
  store uses it.

Once created, the store starts as a **draft**, so you can build it out before
buyers can reach it.

## Give it an identity

Under **Webstore** you choose your store URL (`{slug}.2pay.gg`), display name,
description, accent colour, theme, light or dark appearance, and logo. These
appear on your storefront and at checkout, so they build trust at the moment of
payment. Every setting and its limits are listed in
[Webstore customization](/dev/webstores/customization).

<Callout type="info">
  You go live by submitting your store for review once your email and account
  are verified. See [Going live](/creators/your-store/going-live).
</Callout>
