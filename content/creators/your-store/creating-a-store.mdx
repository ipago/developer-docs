title: Creating your store

---

Your **store** is where you sell. One account can hold several stores, each with
its own catalog, currency, branding, and settings.

## Create a store

From your dashboard, choose **Create store**. You will set:

- A **name** and a **store URL** (its slug), which becomes your storefront address.
- A **currency**, the currency your prices are listed in.

Once created, the store starts in a draft state so you can build it out before
buyers can reach it.

## Give it an identity

In **Settings** you can set a display name, description, accent color, and logo.
These appear on your storefront and in the buyer's checkout, so they build trust
at the moment of payment.

<Callout type="info">
  You go live by submitting your store for review after verification. See
  [Going live](/creators/your-store/going-live).
</Callout>
