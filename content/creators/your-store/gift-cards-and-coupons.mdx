---
title: Gift cards & coupons
description: Run promotions with coupon codes, sales, and gift cards.
---

All three live under **Product Catalog**.

## Coupons

A **coupon** is a code that reduces a basket's total, by a percentage or a fixed
amount. It can apply to the whole store, one category, or one product, and you
can set a minimum basket subtotal, a maximum number of uses, and an expiry date.
Share the code, and buyers enter it in their basket. The panel shows how many
times each code has been redeemed.

## Sales

A **sale** lowers prices without a code: a percentage or fixed discount on the
whole store, a category, or a product, optionally between a start and an end
date. The storefront shows the original price next to the sale price.

## Gift cards

A **gift card** carries a balance a buyer can redeem against purchases in your
store. Issue them from the panel, for example as rewards or compensation.
Redemption draws the balance down at checkout, so a card can never be spent
twice.

A basket can use a coupon or a gift card, not both at once.
