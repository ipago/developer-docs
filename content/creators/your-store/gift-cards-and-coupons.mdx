## Coupons

A **coupon** is a code that reduces a basket's total, either a percentage or a
fixed amount. Create coupons in the panel, share the code, and buyers apply it at
checkout. Use them for launches, seasonal sales, or win-back campaigns.

## Gift cards

A **gift card** carries a balance a buyer can redeem against purchases in your
store. Issue them as rewards or sell them like any other product. Redemption draws
the balance down atomically at checkout, so a card can never be spent twice.
