---
title: Payment methods
description: How 2Pay offers 100+ payment methods and how a store controls which ones appear.
---

2Pay presents buyers the payment methods most likely to convert for their country
and currency, drawn from cards and wallets (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google
Pay), local rails (BLIK, iDEAL, Przelewy24, Bancontact, SEPA), buy-now-pay-later
(Klarna), PayPal, and more. You never touch card data: the hosted checkout
collects and tokenizes it.

## One integration, many methods

Because 2Pay is the Merchant of Record, adding a method is an account-level
setting, not code. Your [checkout](/dev/checkout/overview) automatically offers the
eligible methods for each buyer, so a new market is a configuration change rather
than an integration.

## Per-store selection

A store can narrow the offered set from its settings (for example, cards and BLIK
only). This is materialized per store on the payment provider and applied at
checkout. A store that has not customized the list uses the account default
(dynamic methods chosen per buyer).

<Callout type="info">
  A method only appears if it is both enabled for the store and eligible for the
  buyer's currency and country. Restricting methods too tightly can lower
  conversion for international buyers.
</Callout>
