---
title: Tax & VAT
description: How 2Pay handles sales tax and VAT as Merchant of Record, and inclusive vs exclusive pricing.
---

As Merchant of Record, 2Pay is the legal seller, so **we calculate, collect, and
remit sales tax and VAT**. You never register for tax in the jurisdictions your
buyers are in, and tax is never part of your balance or payout.

## Where tax shows up

Tax is computed at checkout based on the buyer's location and added to the order.
On a paid order you can see the tax that was collected, clearly separated from
your revenue. In the ledger it is booked to a dedicated `tax_payable` liability
account that 2Pay settles with the tax authorities.

## Inclusive vs exclusive

A store chooses, in its settings, how prices are shown:

- **Exclusive** (default): the listed price is pre-tax, and tax is added on top at
  checkout.
- **Inclusive**: the listed price already contains tax, and the tax portion is
  broken out of the total.

Either way, your taxable base (the amount fees are computed on) and your payout
exclude the tax. The only difference is what the buyer sees as the sticker price.

<Callout type="info">
  The platform fee is always computed on the pre-tax base, never on the tax.
</Callout>
