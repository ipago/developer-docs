---
title: Tax & VAT
description: VAT on sales to players, inclusive vs exclusive prices, and the purchase price.
---

iPago Sp. z o.o. is the seller to the player, so **iPago calculates, collects, and
remits VAT** on that sale. You are the supplier. You do not register for VAT in
the player's country for the sale, and player VAT is not part of the purchase
price.

What iPago owes you is the suggested retail price minus the margin, paid on the
weekly self-billing purchase invoice (article 106d of the Polish VAT Act). See
[Invoice payment](/creators/getting-paid/invoices).

## Where tax shows up

VAT is computed when the checkout is created, from the player's country:

- On a [Webstore](/dev/webstores/overview), iPago determines the country itself.
- On `POST /v1/checkouts`, you pass it as `country`, an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code
  such as `"DE"`. If you omit it, the order is treated as non-taxable and no VAT
  is charged, so send it whenever you know where the player is.

On the [order](/dev/developer-api/orders), VAT is reported separately as
`tax_amount`. In the ledger it is booked to a `tax_payable` account. That account
name is the current API. It is not an amount owed to you.

## Inclusive vs exclusive

A store chooses, in its settings, how prices are shown:

- **Exclusive** (default): the listed price is pre-tax, and VAT is added on top at
  checkout.
- **Inclusive**: the listed price already contains VAT, and the VAT portion is
  broken out of it.

Either way, the order's `total` is the net amount excluding VAT, and it is the
base the supplier is paid on: `total` minus the margin (5% of `total` by default)
minus the card cost (2.9% of `total + tax_amount`, plus €0.30 per payment).

<Callout type="info">
  The margin is computed on the net amount, not on the VAT iPago charges the
  player. The card cost is computed on what the player paid, VAT included.
</Callout>
