---
title: Tax & VAT
description: How iPago handles VAT as Merchant of Record, and inclusive vs exclusive pricing.
---

As Merchant of Record, iPago is the legal seller, so **we calculate, collect, and
remit VAT**. You never register for tax in the countries your buyers are in, and
tax is never part of your balance or payout.

## Where tax shows up

VAT is computed when the checkout is created, from the buyer's country:

- On a [Webstore](/dev/webstores/overview), iPago determines the country itself.
- On `POST /v1/checkouts`, you pass it as `country`, an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code
  such as `"DE"`. If you omit it, the order is treated as non-taxable and no VAT
  is charged, so send it whenever you know where the buyer is.

On the [order](/dev/developer-api/orders), VAT is reported separately as
`tax_amount`, apart from your revenue. In the ledger it is booked to a dedicated
`tax_payable` liability account that iPago settles with the tax authorities.

## Inclusive vs exclusive

A store chooses, in its settings, how prices are shown:

- **Exclusive** (default): the listed price is pre-tax, and VAT is added on top at
  checkout.
- **Inclusive**: the listed price already contains VAT, and the VAT portion is
  broken out of it.

Either way, the order's `total` (the base your fees and earnings are computed on)
and your payout exclude VAT. The only difference is what the buyer sees as the
sticker price.

<Callout type="info">
  The platform fee is always computed on the pre-tax base, never on the tax.
</Callout>
