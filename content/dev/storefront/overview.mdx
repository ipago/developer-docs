---
title: Storefront API overview
description: The public, slug-scoped API that powers a store's buyer-facing pages, baskets, and checkout.
---

The **Storefront API** is the public surface a buyer-facing store uses. Every
route is scoped to a store by its slug, `/v1/storefront/{slug}/...`, and serves
the data and actions a shopper needs: browse the catalog, build a basket, and
check out.

Use it to build a custom storefront in front of 2Pay, or to embed buying into your
own site or game. Product and price definitions still live in the panel; the
Storefront API reads them and turns a basket into a paid order.

## What it exposes

- **Store & catalog:** `GET /v1/storefront/{slug}`, `GET /v1/storefront/{slug}/products`,
  `GET /v1/storefront/{slug}/categories`, and a single product by id.
- **Baskets:** create a basket, add and remove items, apply a coupon or gift card,
  then check out. See [Baskets & checkout](/dev/storefront/baskets).
- **Order lookup:** `GET /v1/storefront/{slug}/orders/by-session/{sessionId}` to
  resolve the order after a buyer returns from checkout.
- **Subscriptions:** `POST /v1/storefront/{slug}/subscribe` for recurring products.

<Callout type="info">
  The Storefront API is read-and-buy only. It never creates products or changes
  prices. To manage the catalog, use the panel or the
  [Developer API](/dev/developer-api/overview).
</Callout>
