The **Webstore Builder** lets you fully customize the look of your store with
templates and themes, so customers move seamlessly from your existing website
into a purchase.

New to 2Pay? It's worth reading [Getting Started](/creators/getting-started)
first.

## What you can do

With the Webstore Builder you can:

- **Use templates** to control the HTML structure of each page, with conditional
  logic powered by 2Pay's templating engine.
- **Upload assets**, your own CSS, JavaScript, and images, to style and extend
  your store.
- **Ship configurable templates.** If you design templates for others, you can let
  your customers tweak them through a config schema, and unlock perks through our
  developer program.

## Themes

Start fast with a ready-made theme, then make it your own: colors, layout, and
content. Every store is served on its 2Pay subdomain, and on Creator Plus you can
point it at your own custom domain.

## For template designers

Do you build templates for other creators, whether free or paid?
[Get in touch](mailto:support@2pay.io) and we'll bring you into our designer
program, with insider guides, a private community of designers, feedback sessions
with our product team, and early access to features before they're public.

## Feedback

Need a tag, filter, or variable in your template that isn't available or
documented yet? [Let us know](mailto:support@2pay.io), it directly shapes what we
build next.

## Getting started

To create your first template, open the **Appearance** section in your
[panel](https://app.2pay.gg).
