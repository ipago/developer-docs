Every iPago store gets a **hosted webstore**: a ready-made storefront at
`{slug}.ipago.io` that lists your products, runs the basket, and takes payment.
There is nothing to deploy and no code to write. You shape it from the panel at
[app.ipago.io](https://app.ipago.io), under **Webstore** and **Store settings**.

New to iPago? It's worth reading [Getting Started](/creators/getting-started)
first.

## What buyers get

- **Your catalog**, grouped by your categories, with sale prices shown when a
  sale is running.
- **A basket** with coupon and gift-card codes.
- **A player identifier prompt** when your store requires one, labelled the way
  you choose (for example "Minecraft username").
- **Checkout** on the store's own page, offering the payment methods you enabled,
  with VAT shown for the buyer's country.
- **A confirmation page** showing the order after payment.
- **A footer that names iPago Sp. z o.o.** as the only seller. The player's
  contract is with iPago. The footer does not show the supplier's business name
  or support email.

The webstore runs on the public [Storefront API](/dev/storefront/overview), the
same routes documented there.

## What you can configure

You choose a layout theme, light or dark appearance, accent colour, logo, display
name, description, store URL, and whether buyers must enter a player identifier.
See [Customization](/dev/webstores/customization) for every setting and its
limits.

You cannot upload your own templates, CSS, or JavaScript. Custom templates are
[planned](/dev/webstores/templates), not available.

## Before and after going live

Until iPago approves your store, its URL shows nothing to the public. Owners and
admins can still look at it:

- **Preview storefront** opens the store as buyers will see it, through a
  short-lived preview link.
- **Place a test order** opens it in test mode, with your test-mode products and
  test payments, so you can run a purchase end to end without real money. See
  [Test mode](/dev/storefront/baskets#test-mode).

Once the store is approved it is live at `{slug}.ipago.io` and takes real
payments. Submitting for review is covered in
[Going live](/creators/your-store/going-live).
