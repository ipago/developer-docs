---
title: Custom templates (planned)
description: Custom storefront templates and assets are planned and not available yet.
---

<Callout type="warn">
  **Planned, not available yet.** There is no template engine, template editor,
  or asset upload for webstores today. Nothing on this page can be used yet.
</Callout>

Today a webstore is styled only through the settings in
[Customization](/dev/webstores/customization): one of three layout themes, light
or dark appearance, an accent colour, a logo, and your store's texts.

What is planned is a way to go further: your own page templates, your own CSS,
JavaScript and images, and templates that other creators can reuse. Their syntax
and features are not settled, so they are not documented here.

Want to shape this feature? [Tell us what you need](mailto:support@2pay.gg).
