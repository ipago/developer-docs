---
title: Customization
description: Every setting that changes how your hosted webstore looks and behaves.
---

Owners and admins change these in the panel. Members can see them but not edit
them. Changes reach the live storefront within about a minute, because store
pages are cached for 60 seconds.

## Webstore page

Under **Webstore**, in **Storefront appearance** and **Storefront logo**:

| Setting | What it does | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme | The page layout. **Classic**: a hero banner above a centered grid of packages. **Sidebar**: a sticky category sidebar beside a dense package grid. **Showcase**: a wide banner, a featured row, then packages grouped by category. | One of the three. Defaults to Classic. |
| Appearance | The colour scheme, **Dark** or **Light**. Any theme works with either. | Defaults to Dark. |
| Accent colour | The brand colour used for buttons and highlights. | A `#rrggbb` hex colour, or none. |
| Logo | Shown on the storefront. | PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 1 MB. |
| Display name | The name buyers see. Falls back to the store name. | Up to 120 characters. |
| Description | A short text about your store. | Up to 500 characters. |
| Store URL | The slug that makes your address, `{slug}.ipago.io`. | 3 to 63 characters: lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, not starting or ending with a hyphen. Reserved names such as `www`, `api`, `app`, `admin`, `checkout` or `pay` are refused, and a slug already in use returns "That store URL is taken". |
| Require player identifier | When on, a buyer must enter an identifier before buying, and it becomes the order's delivery target. | On by default. |
| Identifier label | The label on that field, for example "Minecraft username". | Up to 40 characters. |

A store needs a URL before it can be submitted for review.

## Banner and content

Also under **Webstore**, in **Banner and content**:

| Setting | What it does | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banner | A wide image at the top of the storefront home page, under the header. It shows with every theme. | PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 1 MB, like the logo. About 4:1 works best, for example 1600 x 400. |
| Announcement | One line of text in a bar above the header, in your accent colour, on every storefront page. Leave it empty to hide the bar. | Up to 200 characters. Plain text on one line. |
| Content blocks | Your own sections on the home page, each placed **above** or **below** the packages, in the order you set. Add, edit, reorder and remove them in the panel. | Up to 12 blocks. |

There are two kinds of block:

- **Text**: a title and some text. The text is shown as you wrote it: a blank line starts a
  new paragraph and a single line break stays a line break. HTML and markdown are not
  interpreted, so `<b>` or `**bold**` appear as typed. Title up to 120 characters, text up to
  4,000. A text block needs a title or some text.
- **Image**: an image by URL, with an optional title, alt text (what the image shows, read by
  screen readers) and an optional link that the image opens. The image URL and the link must
  be `https://` addresses. Title up to 120 characters, alt text up to 200.

The [storefront object](/dev/reference/storefront#retrieve-a-storefront) returns these as
`banner_url`, `announcement` and `content_blocks`, so a custom frontend can render them too.

## Store settings page

Under **Store settings**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Not shown on the storefront. The footer names iPago Sp. z o.o. as the only seller. |
| Support email | Not shown on the storefront. Players do not see a supplier support email in the footer. |
| Tax display | **Exclusive**: prices are shown before VAT and VAT is added at checkout. **Inclusive**: the price shown already contains VAT. iPago remits VAT on the sale to the player either way. This does not change the purchase price. |
| Payment methods | Which methods the checkout may offer: Card, BLIK, Przelewy24, Klarna, PayPal, iDEAL, Bancontact, and Link. With no selection, iPago's default set applies. A method only shows when it also works for the player's currency and country. |

## Not configurable

- Your own HTML templates, CSS, JavaScript, or uploaded assets. See
  [Custom templates](/dev/webstores/templates).
- A custom domain. The storefront is served on `{slug}.ipago.io` only.
- Page content beyond the settings above, such as extra pages, a custom footer, or formatted
  (HTML or markdown) text in content blocks.
